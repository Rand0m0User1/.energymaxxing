# Submission summary

**The Length of the Night** is a 3D city you can power with solar, wind, batteries, nuclear and gas. You change the season and the time of day, or move the city to the Moon, and see when the lights go out.

**What it shows:** solar is cheap but stops at night. Batteries carry a summer night, but not a long winter one. On the Moon, the night lasts two weeks. The longer the night, the more always-on power, like nuclear, a city needs.

**How to use it:** one panel holds every control. Two short tours walk through the idea: "Day and night" on Earth, and "The Moon".
